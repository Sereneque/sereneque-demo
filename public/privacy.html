Privacy Policy

Sereneque LLC · Effective 28 August 2026

The short version: this website collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no forms, no newsletter, no shopping cart and no payments here. We run no analytics and no advertising trackers, and this site sets no cookies of its own.

What happens when you visit

To show you a page, your browser has to request files, and whoever serves those files necessarily sees your IP address and basic details about your browser. That is how the web works, not a choice we made about you. On this site, those companies are:

  • GitHub Pages — hosts the site and serves every page
  • Google Fonts — serves the typefaces used throughout
  • unpkg — serves a display library used on the Home, Method and Shop pages

We do not receive that information and we have no access to their records of it.

When you leave for the portal

The button on our home page takes you to rx.sereneque.com, a service operated by our partner. That is a separate site with its own privacy practices, and this policy does not cover it. Anything you enter there — including any health information — is handled by them, not by us. Please read their policy before providing information.

Health information

Sereneque LLC is a marketing company. We are not a healthcare provider, a pharmacy or a prescriber. We do not collect, hold or have access to anyone’s medical records or health information.

Children

This site is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct or delete personal information a business holds about you. Because we hold none, there is generally nothing for us to produce or erase — but you are welcome to ask, and we will answer honestly.

If this changes

If we add anything that collects information — a contact form, a newsletter, analytics, advertising — we will update this page and change the date at the top before doing so.
